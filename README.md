## Featured Project

### Real Estate Lead Capture & Qualification Automation

**Status:** Built and tested

**Tools:** n8n, Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail

**The business problem**

Real estate businesses receive enquiries from potential buyers and renters. Manually recording enquiries, notifying agents, and sorting leads can take time.

**My solution**

I built an automation workflow that:

1. Collects customer enquiries through Google Forms.
2. Stores customer information in Google Sheets.
3. Sends an email notification when a new enquiry is received.
4. Sends an acknowledgement email to the customer.
5. Classifies leads according to their submitted property budget.

**Lead qualification rules**

- High priority: ₦50,000,000 and above
- Medium priority: ₦20,000,000 to below ₦50,000,000
- Low priority: Below ₦20,000,000
